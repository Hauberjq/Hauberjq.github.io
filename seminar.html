The Work of Gromov and McDuff on Symplectic 4-Manifolds

Overview

The aim of this working seminar is to understand some of the foundational results of Gromov and McDuff on symplectic 4-manifolds through the theory of pseudoholomorphic curves.

In particular, we want to understand how holomorphic curves can be used to recognize rational and ruled symplectic 4-manifolds, the behaviour of exceptional spheres under deformation, and the symplectic analogue of Castelnuovo's criterion.

Our main reference will be Wendl's notes on holomorphic curves in low-dimensional symplectic topology.
