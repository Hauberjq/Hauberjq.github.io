Research

I work in symplectic topology, with a particular interest in symplectic embedding problems, rational homology balls, and singular Lagrangian submanifolds and their skeleta. I am interested in both quantitative questions, such as symplectic capacities and embedding obstructions, and qualitative questions concerning the rigidity and classification of Lagrangian submanifolds.

Currently I'm thinking a lot about Katok ellipsoids and Vianna tori.
